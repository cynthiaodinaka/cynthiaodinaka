Design Case Study

From Scattered Feed to On‑Brand System

Two Instagram grid transformations, documented step by step — the thinking, the system, and the templates behind each one.

Cynthia Odinaka
Social Media Strategist & Content Systems Designer
2 systems · 24 templates
Context

The Brief

Both clients came to me with the same core problem: strong offers, no visual system. Posts were going up ad hoc — different fonts, clashing colors, no repeatable structure — so nothing looked cohesive enough to build brand recall or make people stop scrolling.

My job was to turn each feed into a system: a locked palette, a repeatable type pairing, and a set of templates the client (or their team) could reuse indefinitely without hiring a designer for every post.

Scope
Full Instagram grid & story template system
Deliverables
24 editable post templates + a brand style guide
My Role
Strategy, palette, typography, template design
Outcome
A plug-and-play system that keeps every post on-brand, without me
Scope Of Work

What I Did & What I Didn’t

So there’s no confusion about my role on either project — this is exactly where my work started and stopped.

✓
What I Did
  • Designed the color palette and typography system for each brand
  • Built the reusable, editable Instagram templates (Canva-ready)
  • Structured the content grid and layout rhythm
  • Wrote a short style guide so the system stays consistent
−
What I Didn’t Do
  • Didn’t manage the account or schedule any posts
  • Didn’t write captions or publish content
  • Didn’t take or select the original photos used in the templates
  • Didn’t run ads, community management, or engagement
Case Study 01

The Moody Lavender System

Client: content creator / coach — goal was a scroll-stopping, batchable feed.
BEFORE
AFTER
›One lavender palette — zero clashing tones across all 12 tiles
›Serif headline + clean sans body, repeated on every template
›“You’ll want to save this” hooks written into the templates

What I Actually Did Here

01
Locked a moody lavender palette
Pulled every photo through the same lavender-toned edit so 12 different shoots read as one cohesive feed.
02
Paired a serif headline with a clean sans body
Same two typefaces on every tile — the pairing itself becomes a recognizable brand signal.
03
Built reusable prompt & weekly-flow templates
Two core layouts the client can duplicate and re-fill in Canva in minutes, no design skill required.
04
Mapped a 12-slide content rhythm
Alternated value posts, behind-the-scenes, and soft-sell so the grid reads intentional at a glance.

Case Study 02

The “Boss Colors” Template System

Client: entrepreneur / small team — goal was a minimalist pack anyone could run alone.
BEFORE
AFTER
›Editable title / subtitle placeholder on every single slide
›One soft, editorial photo tone runs through the whole grid
›Checklist-style template built specifically to earn saves

What I Actually Did Here

01
Simplified to one lavender-and-white system
Stripped the palette down to two tones so the templates stay calm and legible at any size.
02
Built plug-and-play title / subtitle placeholders
Every template has a labeled text slot, so a new post takes minutes, not a design session.
03
Unified the photography tone
Same soft, editorial light and crop ratio across every image slot, even across different shoots.
04
Added a checklist carousel template
A save-worthy format built specifically to lift saves and shares, not just likes.
