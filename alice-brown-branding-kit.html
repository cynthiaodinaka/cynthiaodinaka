01 — The Brief

A Cohesive Identity, Built to Repeat

The goal was a full YouTube branding kit for "Alice Brown," a cottage‑life lifestyle creator — one system flexible enough to cover the banner, channel art, thumbnails, end screens and social layouts, but disciplined enough that every piece reads as the same brand at a glance. Everything below is built from four repeating decisions: a soft cottage‑garden palette, a serif‑and‑sans type pairing, hand‑drawn floral accents, and a consistent tape‑and‑tab motif for calls to action.

PaletteSage green, blush pink and warm cream — pulled straight from a cottage garden, kept to three tones so nothing competes with the photo.
TypographyA soft serif for "Alice Brown" carries the personality; a small letter‑spaced sans handles every label, so the hierarchy never gets confused.
IllustrationHand‑drawn daisies and a single trailing vine soften the grid without ever covering the subject's face.
MotifA recurring "washi tape" tab treats contact info and CTAs as scrapbook elements instead of UI chrome — it's the one device repeated across all 13 assets.

02 — Building The System

From Generic Layout to On‑Brand Banner

Most creator banners start from the same default: a left‑aligned photo, a bold sans headline, a stock button. The system swaps every one of those defaults for something specific to the brand — without losing the layout logic a viewer already expects from a channel banner.
